This project was all about transforming raw accident data into a visually compelling and easy-to-read dashboard. I placed less focus on complex modeling and more on design, layout, and presentation to ensure the insights are clear and impactful.

---

## 🎨 𝐅𝐨𝐜𝐮𝐬 𝐀𝐫𝐞𝐚𝐬
- ✅ Fully interactive dashboard with slicers  
- ✅ Clean and intuitive design & layout for better readability  
- ✅ Graphical storytelling using KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, and pie charts  
- ✅ Thoughtful use of colors, spacing, and formatting to make insights stand out  
- ✅ Built entirely in Microsoft Excel to showcase dashboarding potential beyond just numbers  

---

## 📊 𝐊𝐞𝐲 𝐇𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐥𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐭s
- 🔹 Monthly trend comparison across multiple years  
- 🔹 Casualties segmented by car, bus, truck, bicycle, motorcycle etc.  
- 🔹 Impact of road surface (dry, wet, snow/ice) and light condition (day vs night)  
- 🔹 Urban vs. Rural accident distribution  

---

## 🚀 𝐓𝐚𝐤𝐞𝐚𝐰𝐚𝐲
This dashboard helped me refine my design and presentation skills — showing how thoughtful layout and interactivity can make large datasets easy to interpret and visually engaging.
